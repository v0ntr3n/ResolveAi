## FAQ

**Q: How do I speak to a human?**
A: Say "agent" or press 0 during the phone menu, or type "agent" in chat.

**Q: Can I request the same agent?**
A: Yes, note the agent's name and request them in future interactions.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution?**
A: Ask to speak with a supervisor or escalate to management.

**Q: How do I file a complaint?**
A: Email feedback@resolveai.com with details and order number.
