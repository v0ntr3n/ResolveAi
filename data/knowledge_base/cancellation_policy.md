# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window

### Before Shipment
Orders can be cancelled **free of charge** within the following timeframes:

| Order Status | Cancellation Window | Fee |
|--------------|---------------------|-----|
| Pending | Anytime before processing | Free |
| Processing | Within 2 hours | Free |
| Processing | After 2 hours | $5.00 fee |
| Shipped | Not cancellable | N/A |

### After Shipment
Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You must:
1. Wait for delivery
2. Initiate a return following our [Return Policy](return_policy.md)

## How to Cancel

### Online (Self-Service)
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Order History"
3. Select the order
4. Click "Cancel Order"
5. Confirm cancellation

### Contact Customer Support
- **Phone**: 1-800-RESOLVE (24/7)
- **Email**: support@resolveai.com
- **Live Chat**: Available on website
- **Response Time**: Within 2 hours during business hours

## Automatic Cancellations

Orders are automatically cancelled if:

### Payment Issues
- Payment not received within 48 hours
- Fraud detection flags order
- Payment method declined 3 times

### Inventory Issues
- Item becomes permanently out of stock
- Discontinued product
- Pricing error (you will be notified)

### Shipping Issues
- Cannot ship to provided address
- International shipping restrictions apply
- Hazardous materials restrictions

## Partial Cancellations

### By Customer Request
If you want to cancel only some items:
1. Contact customer support
2. Specify which items to cancel
3. Remaining items will ship as normal
4. Refund issued for cancelled items only

### By Company (Out of Stock)
If some items are unavailable:
1. You will be notified within 24 hours
2. Options presented:
   - Ship remaining items
   - Wait for all items
   - Cancel entire order
3. If no response within 48 hours, order ships with available items

## Refund Processing

| Original Payment | Refund Timeframe |
|------------------|------------------|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5-10 business days |
| PayPal | 3-5 business days |
| Apple Pay/Google Pay | 3-7 business days |
| Gift Card | Immediate (store credit) |
| Klarna/Afterpay | 5-7 business days |

## Special Cases

### Pre-Orders
Pre-orders can be cancelled anytime before the release date:
- Full refund guaranteed
- No cancellation fee
- Must cancel at least 24 hours before shipping

### Subscription Orders
Subscription orders:
- Cancel anytime before next billing date
- No penalties or fees
- Access continues until end of billing period

### Custom/Personalized Items
Custom items CANNOT be cancelled once production begins:
- Production typically starts within 4 hours
- Check order status before cancelling
- If cancellation requested after production, 50% fee applies

## Cancellation Confirmation

After cancellation:
1. **Email confirmation** sent within 15 minutes
2. **SMS notification** (if opted in)
3. **Refund initiated** automatically

If you don't receive confirmation within 1 hour, contact customer support.

## Reordering After Cancellation

To reorder a cancelled order:
1. Check "Cancelled Orders" in account
2. Click "Reorder"
3. Verify payment and shipping
4. Place new order

Note: Inventory availability not guaranteed for reorder.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cancel just one item from my order?**
A: Yes, contact customer support for partial cancellations.

**Q: Will I get a full refund?**
A: Yes, if cancelled within the free cancellation window.

**Q: How do I know if my cancellation was processed?**
A: You'll receive an email confirmation. Check spam/junk folder if not received.

**Q: Can I cancel after the order ships?**
A: No, but you can return the item once delivered following our return policy.

**Q: What if the cancellation button is greyed out?**
A: The order has likely shipped. Check tracking or contact support.
