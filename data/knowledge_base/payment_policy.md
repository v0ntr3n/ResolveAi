# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

### Credit & Debit Cards
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover
- JCB (Japan)

### Digital Wallets
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Samsung Pay

### Bank Transfers
- ACH (Automated Clearing House) - US customers only
- Wire Transfer - For orders over $1,000

### Buy Now, Pay Later
- Klarna (split into 4 interest-free payments)
- Afterpay (split into 4 payments over 6 weeks)

## Payment Security

All transactions are processed through PCI DSS compliant payment processors:
- 256-bit SSL encryption
- 3D Secure authentication for credit cards
- Fraud detection systems monitor all transactions

## Payment Timing

### When Payment is Charged
- **In-stock items**: Charged immediately upon order placement
- **Pre-order items**: Charged when item ships
- **Backordered items**: Charged when item becomes available

### Authorization Holds
For certain orders, we may place a temporary authorization hold:
- This is NOT a charge
- Typically released within 3-5 business days
- Common for large orders or new customers

## Failed Payments

If your payment fails:
1. Check that billing address matches card address
2. Verify CVV/security code is correct
3. Ensure card is not expired
4. Contact your bank if multiple attempts fail

After 3 failed attempts, you must wait 24 hours before trying again.

## Currency & Pricing

- All prices displayed in USD unless otherwise noted
- International orders may be charged in local currency
- Exchange rates are set at time of purchase
- Prices include applicable taxes where required by law

## Refunds to Original Payment Method

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method:
- Credit/debit cards: 5-10 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- Bank transfers: 7-14 business days
- Digital wallets: 3-7 business days

## Partial Payments

For orders using multiple payment methods:
- Refunds are distributed proportionally
- Gift cards are refunded first
- Store credit issued if original method unavailable

## Payment FAQs

**Q: Can I change my payment method after ordering?**
A: No, payment method cannot be changed after order is placed. You must cancel and reorder.

**Q: When will I see the charge on my statement?**
A: Charges typically appear within 1-2 business days, depending on your bank.

**Q: Do you offer payment plans?**
A: Yes, through Klarna and Afterpay for qualifying customers at checkout.
