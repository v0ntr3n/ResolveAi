# Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage

### Standard Warranty
All products come with our standard warranty coverage:

| Product Category | Warranty Period | Coverage |
|------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Electronics | 12 months | Parts & Labor |
| Appliances | 24 months | Parts & Labor |
| Clothing & Accessories | 6 months | Manufacturing Defects |
| Furniture | 12 months | Frame & Structure |
| Software/Digital | 90 days | Functionality |

### Extended Warranty
Available for purchase at checkout:
- Additional 1-3 years coverage
- Covers accidental damage
- Priority customer support
- Express replacement service

## What's Covered

### Manufacturing Defects
- Material defects
- Workmanship issues
- Component failures under normal use
- Design flaws

### Examples of Covered Issues
- Electronic device won't power on
- Appliance motor failure
- Clothing seams coming apart (not from wear)
- Furniture joint failure
- Software bugs affecting core functionality

## What's NOT Covered

### User Damage
- Accidental drops or impacts
- Water damage (unless waterproof rated)
- Screen cracks
- Pet damage
- Normal wear and tear

### Misuse
- Use outside intended purpose
- Unauthorized modifications
- Repairs by non-authorized service
- Commercial use of consumer products
- Failure to follow care instructions

### Cosmetic Issues
- Scratches from normal use
- Fading from sunlight exposure
- Minor color variations
- Surface imperfections not affecting function

## How to File a Warranty Claim

### Step 1: Gather Information
- Order number
- Product serial number (if applicable)
- Photos of the defect
- Description of the issue

### Step 2: Contact Support
- **Online**: Submit claim at resolveai.com/warranty
- **Email**: warranty@resolveai.com
- **Phone**: 1-800-RESOLVE (select option 3)

### Step 3: Assessment
- Initial review within 48 hours
- May request additional photos/video
- Troubleshooting steps provided
- Decision within 5 business days

### Step 4: Resolution
Options include:
1. **Repair** - Free repair by authorized technician
2. **Replacement** - New or refurbished unit
3. **Refund** - Prorated based on age and condition
4. **Store Credit** - Full purchase value

## Repair Process

### In-Warranty Repairs
1. **Ship to Service Center** - Prepaid label provided
2. **Repair Time** - 7-14 business days
3. **Return Shipping** - Free via standard shipping
4. **Tracking** - Email updates at each stage

### Authorized Service Centers
- Must use authorized centers for warranty work
- Unauthorized repairs void warranty
- List of centers: resolveai.com/service-centers

## Replacement Policy

### Eligibility for Replacement
- Same model available
- Issue confirmed as defect
- Within warranty period

### Refurbished Units
- May receive refurbished equivalent
- Same warranty as original
- Equal or better condition guaranteed

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need original packaging for warranty?**
A: No, but original receipt/order confirmation is required.

**Q: Can I get a loaner during repair?**
A: Loaner devices available for extended warranty holders.

**Q: What if I lost my receipt?**
A: We can look up orders using the email address used for purchase.

**Q: Does warranty reset after replacement?**
A: No, warranty continues from original purchase date.

**Q: How long does warranty claim take?**
A: Standard claims: 5-10 business days. Express: 2-3 days (extended warranty).
